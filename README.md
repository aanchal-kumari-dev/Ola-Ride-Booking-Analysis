# 🚖 Ola Ride Booking Analysis

## 💼 Project Overview

This project analyzes ride booking data from Ola to uncover insights related to booking performance, revenue generation, ride cancellations, customer satisfaction, and vehicle utilization.

Using SQL and Power BI, the project transforms raw ride data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and KPI tracking.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

This analysis aims to answer key business questions:

* How many rides were successfully completed?
* What is the total booking revenue generated?
* Which vehicle categories perform best?
* Why are rides being cancelled?
* Which payment methods generate the most revenue?
* How satisfied are customers and drivers?
* What opportunities exist to improve operational efficiency?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### 📈 Booking Performance

* Total Bookings: 103,024
* Successful Bookings: 63,967
* Total Booking Value: ₹57 Million

### 💰 Revenue Insights

* Cash generated the highest revenue at ₹19.3M.
* UPI contributed ₹14.2M, making it the second most preferred payment method.

### 🚗 Vehicle Performance

* Prime Sedan generated the highest booking value at ₹8.3M.
* E-Bike recorded the highest average ride distance.

### ❌ Cancellation Insights

* Nearly 29,000 bookings were cancelled.
* Driver-related issues represented the largest portion of driver cancellations.
* Customers frequently cancelled rides when drivers were not moving toward pickup locations.

### ⭐ Customer Experience

* Customer ratings remained consistently around 4.0 across vehicle categories.
* Driver ratings were also stable, indicating generally positive ride experiences.

---

## 📝 Recommendations

### 🔵 Reduce Driver Cancellations

Implement driver availability verification and vehicle health monitoring to reduce cancellations caused by personal and vehicle-related issues.

### 🔵 Improve Pickup Efficiency

Introduce stricter driver response SLAs and optimize ride allocation to reduce customer cancellations.

### 🔵 Promote Digital Payments

Offer rewards and cashback campaigns to encourage UPI adoption and reduce dependence on cash transactions.

### 🔵 Increase Prime Sedan Availability

Expand supply of high-performing vehicle categories during peak demand periods.

### 🔵 Analyze Cancellation Hotspots

Identify locations and time periods with high cancellation rates to improve operational planning.
